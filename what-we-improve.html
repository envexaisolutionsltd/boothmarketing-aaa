How we choose

A task should earn the right to be automated.

We do not start with a tool list. We test the workflow against practical criteria first.

Repeated

Does the work happen often enough for improvement to matter?

Consistent

Are the rules and inputs clear enough for a reliable workflow?

Material

Is the current handling creating real time cost, delay or risk?

Visible

Can the team understand what the system is doing and where exceptions go?

Maintainable

Can the workflow remain useful after the initial build?

Worthwhile

Is automation genuinely better than simply improving the process?
