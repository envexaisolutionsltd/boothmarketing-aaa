# Booth Marketing Frontend Design Specification

## Visual direction
Preserve the approved design language:
- warm neutral canvas
- deep near-black typography
- muted burgundy and rose accents
- thin divider rules
- generous whitespace
- large editorial headings
- numbered operational systems
- restrained cards
- real workflow diagrams rather than decorative AI imagery

## Avoid
- blue and purple neon AI gradients
- glowing orbs
- robots or digital brains
- circuit patterns
- grid backgrounds
- excessive glass effects
- fake dashboards
- floating 3D objects
- fake testimonials, client logos, statistics or ROI claims
- pricing
- boxes inside boxes

## Motion
Motion should explain behaviour. Use subtle reveals, workflow progression, accordion transitions and mobile navigation movement. Avoid constant background movement and decorative animation.

## Navigation
Desktop navigation uses the full viewport width. Mobile navigation opens as a right-side drawer at approximately 75% of viewport width.

## FAQ
Only one FAQ item may be open at a time.

## About
Founder content appears near the end of the sales journey. Use a genuine founder photograph in production rather than generated imagery.

## Conversion
Primary CTA: Request an Automation Audit.
Secondary CTA: See What We Automate.

The visitor should always understand what happens after clicking the primary CTA and should not need to know what they want automated before contacting Booth Marketing.
