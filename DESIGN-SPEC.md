# Booth Marketing Frontend Design Specification

## Source of truth
The approved visual reference is the supplied Booth Marketing Vercel screenshots from 1 September 2026.

## Visual direction
- near-black background
- bold modern sans-serif headings
- off-white primary text
- muted grey supporting copy
- cream rectangular CTA buttons with small corner radius
- muted red accent used sparingly
- thin low-contrast borders
- large amounts of vertical whitespace
- subtle rounded dark cards
- real Booth script logo with rose in header and footer
- operational diagrams rather than decorative AI imagery

## Homepage structure
1. Hero with operational positioning
2. Manual handling vs clearer operational flow panel
3. Four operational pain cards
4. Process-problem statement bar
5. Three-card approach section
6. How We Work CTA strip
7. Centred Automation Audit CTA
8. Logo-led footer

## Internal buyer persona
Daniel Mercer, 42, is the fictional Managing Director of a 28-person Manchester B2B facilities and compliance company. He already uses normal business software. His problem is that staff manually connect systems, move information, chase updates, and keep processes working through memory.

The website should make a buyer like Daniel think: "These people understand why running my company feels harder than it should."

## Avoid
- blue or purple neon AI gradients
- robots or digital brains
- circuit patterns
- grid backgrounds
- excessive glass effects
- fake dashboards
- floating 3D objects
- fake testimonials, client logos, statistics, or ROI claims
- pricing
- unnecessary nested boxes
- AI jargon before the operational problem is understood

## Navigation
Desktop navigation uses the full available width. Mobile navigation opens as a right-side drawer at approximately 75% of viewport width.

## FAQ
Only one FAQ item may be open at a time.

## About
Founder content appears near the end of the sales journey. Do not use AI-generated founder imagery.

## Conversion
Primary CTA: Request an Automation Audit.
The visitor should not need to know what they want automated before contacting Booth Marketing.
